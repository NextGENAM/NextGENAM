About the Centre

Driving research excellence in materials & additive manufacturing

The NextGen Materials and Additive Manufacturing Research Centre is a Special Interest Group under the Department of Mechanical Engineering at RNS Institute of Technology. Our group brings together faculty working across advanced materials, additive manufacturing, and manufacturing simulation to drive research at the intersection of next-generation materials and modern fabrication techniques.

We work on problems spanning material design, processing, characterization, and optimization — with an emphasis on interdisciplinary collaboration, sustainable manufacturing practices, and translating research into real-world industry applications.
